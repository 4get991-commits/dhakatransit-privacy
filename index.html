Privacy Policy for dhakatransit

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how dhakatransit ("we", "our", or "us") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

dhakatransit is designed as a local public transit directory app to help users navigate local bus routes and transit schedules. We respect your privacy and do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to external servers.

  • Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • Location Data: The app does not track or store your real-time background location. Any transit lookup features operate locally on your device.
  • Device & Usage Data: The app operates offline/locally and does not gather diagnostic logs or analytics data.

2. How We Use Data

Since no personal data is collected or stored on external servers, your data remains completely on your device. Local data structures and preferences stored within the app are strictly used to provide route navigation and search functionality.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data with third parties or advertising networks.

4. Data Retention and Security

All app-related settings and transit lookups remain strictly on your local mobile device. Uninstalling the app will completely remove any app data stored on your device.

5. Children's Privacy

dhakatransit does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
